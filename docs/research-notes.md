# Research notes: how a modern drilling rig on the Norwegian shelf works

Research done 29 Oct 2026. The facts below are the basis for the video script.
Each item lists its source. Items marked **[engineering]** are standard drilling-engineering
knowledge, not tied to one news source.

---

## 1. The setting: the Norwegian Continental Shelf (NCS)

- 2025 activity: **49 exploration wells terminated, 40 of them wildcats, 21 discoveries**.
  It was the second-best exploration year in ten years. **175 development wells were spudded**,
  one of the busiest years since the 2015 peak. About 40 exploration wells are expected in 2026.
  Sources: [Sodir resource report 2026, exploration](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2026/exploration/),
  [Sodir: The shelf in 2025](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/the-shelf/the-shelf-in-2025/summary/)
- Regulators were renamed on 1 Jan 2024. The Petroleum Safety Authority became **Havtil** (Norwegian Ocean
  Industry Authority). The Norwegian Petroleum Directorate became **Sodir** (Norwegian Offshore Directorate).
  Sources: [Havtil: PSA to change its name](https://www.havtil.no/en/explore-technical-subjects2/technical-competence/news/2023/psa-to-change-its-name/),
  [Sodir announcement](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2023/the-norwegian-petroleum-directorate-and-the-petroleum-safety-authority-are-changing-their-names)
- Harsh weather: the **Draupner wave** (1 Jan 1995) was 25.6 m crest-to-trough, in a sea with a significant
  wave height of about 12 m. It was the first measured rogue wave.
  Source: [Wikipedia: Draupner wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draupner_wave)
- Barents Sea: **Johan Castberg** started production 31 Mar 2025. It has 30 wells on 10 subsea templates
  plus 2 satellites, tied back to an FPSO with a 220,000 bbl/d plateau.
  Source: [Equinor: Johan Castberg](https://www.equinor.com/energy/johan-castberg)

## 2. Rig types and the newest rigs

- Three kinds of rig drill on the NCS:
  - Fixed-platform drilling rigs, e.g. the Johan Sverdrup drilling platform.
  - Harsh-environment jack-ups, e.g. Askepott, Maersk/Noble Intrepid and Integrator.
  - Harsh-environment **semi-submersibles**, which do most floating work. **[engineering]**
- **Cat D rigs** (Transocean Enabler, Encourage, Endurance, Equinox) are semi-submersibles built to Equinor's order,
  adapted to Norwegian winter conditions, Daewoo 2016. Their main figures:

  | Item | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Derrick | MH Wirth, 52 m tall, offline stand building |
  | Hook load | 750 st (680 t) |
  | Compensator | 750 st static / 500 st compensating, plus active heave compensation |
  | Accommodation | 130 beds |
  | Mud system | 7,500 psi, with a 15,000 psi choke and kill manifold |
  | Power | 6 × Wärtsilä 12V32E engines = 37.8 MW |
  | Hull | ~116 m long, displacement 60,124 t, max operating draft 25.15 m |
  | Water depth | up to 500 m |

  The Encourage spec sheet lists Kongsberg DP3 positioning plus 8 mooring lines (POSMOOR ATA).
  Source: [Transocean Enabler rig sheet](https://www.deepwater.com/Documents/RigSpecs/Transocean%20Enabler.pdf),
  [Transocean Encourage rig sheet](https://www.deepwater.com/Documents/RigSpecs/Transocean%20Encourage.pdf)
- **June 2026:** Equinor and Transocean agreed a deal worth about **USD 1 billion** for three Cat D rigs running towards 2035.
  - Endurance: 2-year program from Q2 2027.
  - Enabler: 3-year program from Q1 2028.
  - Encourage: 2-year program from Q1 2028.

  Source: [World Oil 30 Jun 2026](https://www.worldoil.com/news/2026/6/30/transocean-lands-seven-year-1-billion-equinor-rig-deal/),
  [Offshore Energy](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/2026/07/01/transoceans-rig-trio-scoops-up-1-billion-for-drilling-work-in-norwegian-waters)
- **Jan 2026:** Noble entered the Norwegian floater market. It won a 3-year Aker BP contract for the harsh-environment semi
  *Noble GreatWhite* (about USD 473 m), with work from Q2 2027.
  Source: [Noble press release](https://noblecorp.com/press-release/noble-corporation-plc-announces-new-awards-totaling-1-3-billion-and-strategic-entry-into-the-norwegian-floater-market/)
- The newest semis on the shelf were delivered in 2019–2020. Transocean Norge is a Moss CS60, 6th generation.
  Deepsea Bollsta is a Moss CS60E. Odfjell bought Bollsta in Dec 2025 for USD 480 m and renamed it **Deepsea Bergen** in 2026.
  Sources: [Transocean Norge rig sheet](https://www.deepwater.com/Documents/RigSpecs/Transocean%20Norge.pdf),
  [OE Digital](https://www.oedigital.com/news/533506-deepsea-bollsta-rig-gets-new-name-as-odfjell-drilling-takes-ownership)
- Deepsea Atlantic is a dual-derrick, dynamically positioned rig of enhanced GVA 7500 design. Sister rigs are Deepsea Stavanger
  and Deepsea Aberdeen. Equipment includes:
  - 4 × NOV 14-P-220 mud pumps, at 2,200 hp each.
  - A Cameron EVO 15k BOP.
  - 8 mooring lines.
  - An active-heave drawworks.

  Source: [Odfjell: Deepsea Atlantic](https://odfjelldrilling.com/rig/deepsea-atlantic)

## 3. Record wells and geosteering

- **Aker BP, Omega Alfa (Yggdrasil area), 2025:** drilled from **Deepsea Stavanger**. One multilateral exploration well
  hit five targets with branches 25/1-14 and A–G. It drilled about **45,000 m in total, 40,000 m of it in reservoir**,
  in about three months. Its longest branch is **10,666 m**, the longest wellbore ever drilled on the NCS, at a
  vertical depth of only about 1,990 m. Recoverable volumes rose to 96–134 mmboe.
  Sources: [Aker BP stock exchange notice](https://akerbp.com/borsmelding/significant-oil-discovery-in-the-yggdrasil-area/),
  [Sodir: Some of the wells that made a difference](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2026/some-of-the-wells-that-made-a-difference/)
- **Troll S-23 (2021):** 10,042 m, the longest oil well ever drilled from a floating rig.
  Source: [Equinor industriminne: World-record well on Troll](https://equinor.industriminne.no/en/world-record-well-on-troll/)
- Ultra-deep azimuthal resistivity (UDAR / "reservoir mapping while drilling") maps the reservoir in 3D around the bit.
  Equinor has helped develop it for a decade. New tools claim look-ahead up to about 100 m ahead of the bit.
  Sources: [SLB case study: GeoSphere 360, Equinor North Sea](https://www.slb.com/resource-library/case-study/dr3/geosphere-360-equinor-north-sea-cs),
  [SLB SPWLA 2026 paper](https://www.slb.com/resource-library/technical-paper/dr/spwla-2026-0139)
- **Wired drill pipe** sends signals about **10,000× faster** than mud-pulse telemetry. Equinor holds frame agreements
  with NOV and SLB (IntelliServ), and Reelwell DualLink is used at Oseberg.
  Source: [Equinor: wired drill pipe contracts](https://www.equinor.com/en/news/2018-12-04-wired-drill-pipe.html),
  [OE Digital: Reelwell](https://www.oedigital.com/news/503423-reelwell-to-provide-equinor-with-duallink-powered-and-wired-drill-pipe-in-10m-deal)

## 4. Automation, AI and remote operations

- **Feb 2025: the world's first automated on-bottom drilling system**, on an Equinor well on the NCS. It combines
  Halliburton LOGIX automation and remote operations with Sekal Drilltronics and the rig's control system.
  The closed loop steers the well, mitigates vibration and optimises hole cleaning, while Drilltronics safeguards
  stay active.
  Source: [Halliburton press release](https://www.halliburton.com/en/about-us/press-release/halliburton-sekal-deliver-world-first-automated-on-bottom-drilling-system-equinor-north-sea),
  [Sekal](https://sekal.com/halliburton-and-sekal-deliver-worlds-first-automated-on-bottom-drilling-system-for-equinor-in-the-north-sea/)
- **Remote operations centres:** Equinor moved directional drilling, cementing and fluids engineering roles from the
  Askepott rig to onshore centres in Stavanger and Tananger, roughly halving the service crew offshore.
  Hands-on work stays on the rig.
  Source: [Baker Hughes: Pass the remote](https://www.bakerhughes.com/company/energy-forward/pass-remote-bringing-equinors-offshore-roles-land)
- **Mar 2026:** Aker BP and Armada will put an offshore modular data centre on the NCS for real-time, AI-driven
  drilling analytics. Source: [World Oil](https://worldoil.com/news/2026/3/23/aker-bp-armada-to-deploy-offshore-modular-data-center-for-ai-driven-drilling-operations/)
- **Yggdrasil (Aker BP):** its platforms will be operated from an onshore control room in Stavanger from 2027.
  Source: [Offshore Technology](https://www.offshore-technology.com/projects/yggdrasil-field-development-north-sea-norway/)
- **Robots on the drill floor:** Robotic Drilling Systems (Stavanger) started with Equinor money in 2008 and is now
  Nabors/Canrig. Its robot finished a 5-month offshore test on a North Sea semi. NOV's ATOM RTX arms are being ordered
  for land and offshore rigs. I found no confirmation that robot arms are in routine use on a Norwegian rig in 2026,
  so the video calls them *being trialled and rolled out*.
  Sources: [JPT: A robot takes over the drilling floor](https://jpt.spe.org/a-robot-takes-over-the-drilling-floor),
  [OGJ: Drill-floor robots promote operational safety](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/article/14235410/drill-floor-robots-promote-operational-safety),
  [Drilling Contractor](https://drillingcontractor.org/advanced-robotics-enable-revolution-on-the-rig-floor-74442)
- **Hands-free pipe handling** on Norwegian rigs (pipe racker, iron roughneck, red zone) came from Norwegian
  operators' safety and manning rules. Offshore Norge guideline 081 requires a "red zone" on the drill floor.
  Sources: [Offshore Norge guideline 081](https://www.offshorenorge.no/contentassets/e40ae11c682f429b9e8241b881e0e920/081---recommended-guidelines-for-remote-pipe-handling-operations-final-1406192.pdf),
  [IADC](https://iadc.org/dcpi/dc-sepoct02/Sep2-auto.pdf)
- **May 2026:** Equinor extended its integrated drilling and well-services contracts with Baker Hughes, Halliburton and SLB
  (NOK 8.3 bn), bringing the total with specialist frameworks to about NOK 17 bn.
  Source: [Drilling Contractor](https://drillingcontractor.org/baker-hughes-halliburton-slb-win-equinor-drilling-and-well-services-contracts-on-norwegian-shelf-78125)

## 5. Power and emissions

- Hybrid battery rigs on the NCS:
  - **Transocean Spitsbergen**: 5.6 MW battery system, about 15% less fuel in DP3.
  - **Maersk Intrepid**: about 25% less CO2 and about 95% less NOx.
  - The **NOx Fund** pays up to 80% of the cost.

  Sources: [gCaptain](https://gcaptain.com/transocean-semi-submersible-is-first-to-use-battery-power-for-drilling-operations/),
  [OE Digital](https://www.oedigital.com/news/483990-first-results-are-in-maersk-drilling-s-hybrid-jack-up-cuts-co2-emissions-by-25)
- Odfjell is retrofitting Siemens Energy **BlueDrive DC-grid** systems, the first on an offshore drilling rig.
  Deepsea Atlantic has a 564 kWh battery system.
  Source: [OE Digital: Siemens to upgrade Odfjell rigs](https://www.oedigital.com/news/484864-siemens-to-upgrade-odfjell-drilling-s-offshore-rigs-to-minimize-emissions)
- **Power from shore:** Askepott was electrified (by KCA Deutag's Kenera, with a 6 MW static converter) to become the first rig in
  Equinor's portfolio powered from shore. Power comes via Martin Linge A, over a roughly 162 km AC cable.
  The expected saving is about 20,000 t CO2 a year. Q4 2024 was the target; I found no public confirmation
  of commissioning, so the script says "expected".
  Source: [Offshore Energy](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/kca-deutag-in-charge-of-electrification-project-enabling-equinor-to-get-its-first-onshore-powered-rig/)
- **Johan Sverdrup** runs on shore power (300 MW after phase 2) at about **0.67 kg CO2 per barrel**. Phase 3 was approved
  in 2025. Source: [Norskpetroleum: Johan Sverdrup](https://www.norskpetroleum.no/en/facts/field/johan-sverdrup/)
- **Hywind Tampen:** 11 floating turbines (88 MW) supply about 35% of the power for Gullfaks A/B/C and Snorre A/B,
  cutting about 200,000 t CO2 a year. Source: [gCaptain](https://gcaptain.com/norway-opens-worlds-largest-floating-offshore-wind-farm)

## 6. Well integrity and well control

- **NORSOK D-010, the two-barrier principle:** there must always be two independent well barrier envelopes.
  - Primary barrier while drilling: the hydrostatic column of drilling fluid.
  - Secondary barrier: casing, cement, wellhead and the BOP.

  Sources: [Offshore Norge well integrity intro](https://offshorenorge.no/globalassets/dokumenter/drift/presentasjonerarrangementer/well-integrity-workshop-2013/09_introductionwellintegrity2013.pdf),
  [SPE: Updated NORSOK D-010](https://file.streaming.spe.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/22133740/Updated-NORSOK-Standard-D-010.pdf)
- A subsea BOP for the NCS is 18¾" and rated 15,000 psi (e.g. Cameron EVO 15k). **[engineering]**
  - It has annular preventers, pipe rams and blind/casing shear rams.
  - It has redundant control pods, plus ROV and acoustic backup.
  - It has an emergency disconnect sequence (EDS) for DP rigs.

  Havtil's facilities regulations point to NORSOK D-001 for compensators and disconnection.
  Source: [Havtil facilities regulations §50](https://www.havtil.no/en/regulations/all-acts/the-facilities-regulations3/VIII/50/)
- **Managed pressure drilling:** Equinor has a 5-year frame agreement with Enhanced Drilling (2024). It covers:
  - RMR (riserless mud recovery).
  - EC-Drill controlled mud level, which uses a subsea pump on the riser.
  - EC-Drill Dual MPD.
  - MPC cementing.

  RMR let a 20" shoe go deeper, which removed a 16" liner.
  Source: [Enhanced Drilling](https://blog.enhanced-drilling.com/news/enhanced-drilling-wins-5-year-frame-agreement-for-all-their-technologies-with-equinor)
- **Top-hole innovation:** Neodrill's Conductor Anchor Node (CAN) is a suction-anchored well foundation installed before
  the rig arrives. The company claims up to 44% lower top-hole environmental impact.
  Source: [JPT](https://jpt.spe.org/conductor-anchor-node-optimizes-efficiency-riserless-deepwater-exploration-drilling)

## 7. Drilling waste

- Norway banned discharge of oil-based-mud cuttings in 1993. Cuttings with more than 1% oil by weight may not be discharged.
  Oil-based cuttings are re-injected, shipped to shore ("skip and ship") or thermally cleaned. In the Barents Sea,
  only top-hole discharges are allowed. Sources: [Ekofisk industriminne: Drill cuttings](https://ekofisk.industriminne.no/en/2329-2/),
  [OE Digital: Treating drilling waste offshore](https://www.oedigital.com/news/459927-treating-drilling-waste-offshore)

## 8. People

- The typical Norwegian rotation is **2 weeks offshore, 4 weeks off**, with 12-hour shifts (07–19 / 19–07), 7 days a week.
  Crews fly out by helicopter. Source: [Draugen industriminne: Working life on Draugen](https://draugen.industriminne.no/en/2018/10/10/working-life-on-draugen/)
- A drill crew is typically made up of **[engineering]**:
  - Rig and service-company staff: the OIM, toolpusher, driller, assistant driller, derrickman (pipe-handling operator),
    roughnecks, crane operators and mechanics.
  - Operator and service staff: the drilling supervisor (company man), mud engineer, mud loggers,
    directional driller and MWD engineer. Some of these now work from shore.

## 9. Standard engineering figures used in the animation [engineering]

- A typical NCS casing programme:
  - 30"/36" conductor
  - 20" surface casing
  - 13⅜" intermediate casing
  - 9⅝" production casing
  - 7" liner, or screens in the reservoir
- Top hole is drilled riserless, with returns to the seabed or via RMR. The BOP and riser are run before the
  12¼" / 8½" sections.
- Mud circulation runs in this order: mud pits → mud pumps (about 7,500 psi) → standpipe → top drive →
  drill string → bit nozzles → annulus → riser → flowline → shale shakers → degasser → desander and desilter →
  centrifuges → back to the pits.
- What the mud does:
  - Cools and lubricates the bit.
  - Carries the cuttings up.
  - Holds back formation pressure. This is the primary barrier.
  - Stabilises the hole wall with filter cake.
  - Carries the MWD mud-pulse signal.
- The BHA (bottom-hole assembly), from the bottom up:
  - PDC bit
  - rotary steerable system (push-the-bit / point-the-bit)
  - MWD / LWD tools (gamma, resistivity, density/neutron, sonic, pressure-while-drilling)
  - drill collars
  - jar
  - heavy-weight drill pipe
  - drill pipe
- Telemetry speeds:
  - Mud-pulse telemetry: about 1–20 bit/s.
  - Wired pipe: up to 57,600 bit/s.
- The kick sequence:
  1. Detect the kick: pit gain, flow increase.
  2. Space out, then shut in the annular or pipe rams.
  3. Read SIDPP / SICP.
  4. Circulate the kick out through the choke, using the Driller's or Wait-and-Weight method.
- Heave compensation keeps the bit steady while the floater moves several metres:
  - Active-heave drawworks or a crown/in-line compensator.
  - Riser tensioners plus a telescopic slip joint.
- Make-up torque: the iron-roughneck readout in the film (52,000 N·m, about 38,000 ft-lbf) is an illustrative
  value, in line with the narration's "tens of thousands of newton-metres". Real values depend on pipe size, grade
  and connection. For example, Odfjell Technology's 5⅞" XT57 heavy-weight drill pipe sheets list maximum make-up
  torques of roughly 53,000–64,000 ft-lbf (about 72–86 kN·m).
  Source: [Odfjell Technology HWDP 5⅞" spec sheet](https://www.odfjelltechnology.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/hwdp-5-875-05-spec-sheet.pdf)
